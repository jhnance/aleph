# Decisions — 2026-06-12

## Invite flow: consent-gated invitations riding the magic-link machinery

**Context:** Review checklist 4.1 — promote the org-join open question (invite link vs. direct email add vs. self-join) to a designed use case.

**Decision:** Invitation-only, consent-gated. An admin+ invites `{ email, role }` (`viewer|member|admin`; `owner` not invitable); a pending `org_invitations` row grants nothing until acceptance creates the membership. Acceptance paths: the invite link (unauthenticated atomic redemption that also signs the invitee in — reusing the magic-link token shape: 32 bytes, SHA-256, single-use `UPDATE ... RETURNING`) or explicit accept from the org-selection / create-org screens, which surface pending invitations; nothing auto-converts at sign-in. Resend = revoke + fresh row; rows are never deleted (tombstones `accepted_at`/`revoked_at`); one live invitation per (org, email) via partial unique index; creation is rate-limited and anti-enumeration-uniform. RLS: three-plane policy (admin org branch, invitee email branch, capability-keyed `app.invite_code_hash` redemption branch) with access matrix in `data-model.md`. Domain auto-join deferred post-MVP (ALIGNMENT.md non-goal).

**Rationale:** In a magic-link system email ownership *is* identity, so "send invite link" and "add email directly" converge — except on consent, and consent-gating costs nothing extra while avoiding memberships nobody asked for. Reusing the auth machinery means no second token system to secure. The membership insert happens as the invitee (user-id branch of the memberships policy), resolving the 3.2 follow-up with no policy change. Direct-add and auto-join remain possible later as additive flows.
